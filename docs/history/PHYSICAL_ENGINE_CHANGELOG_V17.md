# Genny 0.17 — Piano String Coupling

## Added

- `PianoHammerAction`: nonlinear felt contact `F = K x^p`, hammer mass, contact time and rebound diagnostics.
- Two transverse polarization planes for every piano string in `PianoStringCourse`.
- Weak reduced longitudinal string mode coupled through the bridge.
- Note-position-dependent local bridge mobility through `FrequencyDependentBridge.step_note_forces()`.
- New diagnostics: `polarization_energy()`, `longitudinal_energy()`, `last_note_velocities`, hammer force/contact/rebound state.

## Changed

- `PolyphonicPiano` now sends per-note forces into one shared bridge and receives note-dependent local bridge velocities.
- `una corda` reduces both physical hammer-force energy and normalized realtime excitation.
- Hammer excitation is generated by a nonlinear contact simulation rather than only a synthetic half-sine pulse.

## Model scope

The polarization, longitudinal and local-bridge models are reduced-order realtime surrogates. They preserve the intended coupling topology and causal energy exchange but are not a full 3D FEM/BEM piano model.

## Validation

The targeted physical/backward-compatibility suite from v0.6 through v0.17 plus `test_spec.py` passes: **67 tests**.
