# What genny models, and how

One row per physical phenomenon: the model used, the catalog names that reach it, the module, and the
source the model was ported from. Each module page (`docs/<module>.md`) gives parameters, recipes, the
numbers measured by its tests and its unsourced constants. `docs/catalog.md` lists every name with its
parameters; `docs/api.md` is the Python API.

"Measured" in the module pages means a test checks the model against its own physics (mode frequency,
decay time, pitch, event rate). Nothing here was validated by listening tests.

## Wind acoustics (`genny.physical.winds`)

Analytic wind acoustics, all in SI units: air constants and bore losses, tone-hole lengths (Lefebvre, Dubos,
Keefe), the lattice cutoff, closed-form inharmonicity of a woodwind bore (cents = IH/5.78e-4), transfer-matrix
input impedance with hole optimisation (`tmm_impedance`), brassiness, measured lip and reed tables (Cullen Table
A/B, Newton et al., Adachi Table I-III, Buffet Crampon clarinet: `buffet_clarinet`), flue-jet laws (expert
controls, Strouhal regimes, register hysteresis of an alto recorder: `register_track`), `lip_threshold`, and
self-oscillating models: `modal_clarinet` (cubic reed on bore modes), `trombone_lip` / `lip_buzz` (one-mass lip
on modal bore resonances), `trumpet_lip2d` (Adachi's 2D lip) and `auvray_recorder` (jet drive on the Zen-On
recorder's window admittance).

- **Playing pitch of a lip or reed instrument.** An outward-striking valve (lips) sounds above its own resonance
  and above the bore peak; an inward-striking one (reed) sounds below both. The note sits on the bore peak, pulled
  toward the valve resonance by an amount that grows as the valve Q falls.
- **Flue jets.** Disturbances travel along the jet at 0.4-0.92 of the jet speed (convection ratio);
  `recorder(convection=)` scales the jet delay by 0.4/convection. Register hysteresis of a flue pipe
  (`foley.flue_register`, sfx `overblow`): up at 311.5 Pa + Pcorr, back down at 259 Pa − Pcorr, Pcorr =
  (3 |dP/dt| / 2α)^(2/3), so a fast crescendo jumps later; below 224 Pa the jet only whistles.
- **Waveguides.** `waveguides.brass(inward=, inward_ratio=1.3)` adds an inward-striking lip resonance.
  `waveguides.clarinet(reflection="pasp", legato=)`: one-pole bell (a1 = v(t) − 0.642 with a 5 Hz, 0.03 vibrato)
  and a crossfade of two read pointers on a pitch step. `waveguides.recorder(model="flute")` takes `jet_offset`
  (y/h), `jet_angle` (deg), `jet_shape="tanh"`, `jet_filter="butter"`, `convection`, `register=True` (register
  hysteresis, breath 1 = 400 Pa) and `skill`; `model="auvray"` renders `winds.auvray_recorder`.
- **Impedance clarinet.** `impedance.clarinet(bore="cone", cone_angle=2.0, hardness=, bite=, reed_age=, wet=,
  bend=, squeak=, reed_flow=, tonehole_fc=)`; `stiffening=True` works with every reed.
- **Tone-hole networks.** `networks.DynamicToneHole` is an impedance at `f_ref` from Lefebvre's open and closed
  hole lengths (closed chimney = quarter-wave stub, anti-formant at c/4(t + tm));
  `MultiHoleBore(f_ref_hz=0 → c/4L, nonlinear_holes=)`.
- **Lips and voice.** `LipBoreInteraction` takes its embouchure from the lip channel b·H0 of Cullen's Table B.
  `voice.lf_pulse(noise_shift=)` moves the aspiration hump toward the closing phase (0.35 → 60 % of the cycle).
- **Catalogue options.** `organ` `registration` (nine drawbar digits 0-8: 16', 5 1/3', 8', 4', 2 2/3', 2',
  1 3/5', 1 1/3', 1'; 3 dB per step, e.g. `888000000`) and `key_click` 0..1; `brass follow=1`: the filter cutoff
  follows the amplitude envelope, f (1.5 + 10 bright vel env), with a 2 % pitch scoop.

## Known limits

- **Bowed strings and brass in the default catalog are hybrids.** `violin`, `cello`, `trumpet`, `trombone`,
  `french_horn`, `tuba` and `flute` mix the waveguide with an additive voice at the note's pitch, because the
  raw waveguides do not hold pitch across their range. `bowed_string_fd` is fully physical and in tune but
  takes 0.1-0.4 s to speak at ordinary bow force.
- **Nonlinear plates are flat rectangles**, not curved shells: the cymbals are darker than real ones.
- **`timpani`, `glockenspiel`, `tubular_bell`** add a sine at the note's pitch to a modal body that is not
  tuned to it.
- **No 3-D geometry**: no finite-element modes of arbitrary meshes, no fluid simulation; the fire and water
  models are driven by stochastic surrogates of the simulations in the source papers.
- **Spring reverb**: `spring_reverb` is a dispersive allpass loop (Välimäki-Parker-Abel stretched allpasses); its
  `model="fd"` is the wire as Fletcher's two coupled wave equations, slow, not the book's helical-spring scheme.
- Constants without a source are marked `# UNSOURCED` in the code and listed at the end of each module page.
